Process

Why these videos stay in sync — and stay revisable

01

Script first, approved before anything moves

You sign off the script sentence-by-sentence. It becomes the backbone: every animation beat references a specific spoken word.

02

Measured narration, not eyeballed timing

The voice-over is transcribed with word-level timestamps, and the waveform is analyzed for real sentence boundaries. Visuals land on words, not on guesses.

03

Code-driven animation (Remotion)

Every video is a React component in version control. Copy tweak on frame 400? Re-render in minutes — no timeline surgery.
